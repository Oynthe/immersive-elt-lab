Camera-based augmented reality

The Augmented City Experience

Your camera looks at a printed page of Lindenbrück. Live information, audio and a 3D museum object appear on top of it and stay attached to the page as you move.

  1. Show a target on a second screen (laptop, tablet, a groupmate's shared screen) or print it. In the portal, open AR Explorer and choose a target.
  2. Start the camera on this device and allow camera access.
  3. Point the camera at the whole page from about 30 to 50 cm. Open at least two overlay items.

Privacy: the camera image is analysed on your device only. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.
