Which device do I need?

  • Phone and laptop: show a target on the laptop, scan it with the phone.
  • Phone only: use the 360° viewer on the phone. For AR, scan a printed target or a groupmate's screen, or use the non-camera version.
  • Laptop only: use the 360° viewer. For AR, hold a printed target up to the webcam, or use the non-camera version.
  • No camera permission, no printer, slow connection: the static and non-camera versions carry the same content.

The camera image is processed on your device only. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded, and this site sets no cookies and collects no personal data.
