The Augmented City Experience: non-camera version

Accessible alternative to camera-based AR
This page is not augmented reality. Nothing here is attached to a real object through a camera. It is a 2D walkthrough of the same overlay content, for participants who have only one device, no camera, restricted camera permission or limited bandwidth, or who use a screen reader. Open the camera version instead.

In the camera version, the numbered items below float above the printed page and stay attached to it as the phone moves, and the museum jar appears as a 3D object that can be viewed from all sides. Keep that difference in mind when you analyse what AR adds. Items marked "Live information" stand for data that would change in a real service; in this workshop their content is scripted and does not change.
